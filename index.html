The Vision

Built for exactly this

Hamilton House can become the trusted gateway to a world of health and performance — interactive, personalized, and connected to a real community. Not just content. A living experience that grows with every member interaction.

The Foundation

Laird and Gabby have spent a lifetime learning. They are surrounded by extraordinary people — trainers, physicians, recovery experts, nutritionists. What makes Hamilton House special is not that they claim to have every answer. It is that they know, trust, and learn from people who do.

That trust is the product.

The Opportunity

Someone experiences Hamilton House at a resort in Hawaii or Oregon. They go home, continue the practice inside the app, stay connected to the community, book another visit — and keep learning from Laird, Gabby, and the people they trust.

That loop is the business. And fig1 is how you build it without starting over every time you learn something.

Our Approach

A different kind of partnership

Hamilton House should not spend $70,000 to get an app framework. We built fig1 to eliminate that model entirely.

01

Get the experience live

Give us the content and we will get the initial Hamilton House experience up across web and mobile without a traditional build fee. It is an important showcase for us too — we earn the right to build more by delivering first.

No upfront build cost
02

Build the ongoing relationship

Our business together is built around things that require real expertise: content production, new functionality, integrations, analytics, optimization, commerce, and whatever custom work Hamilton House needs next.

Earn as we deliver
03

Work alongside Moe

Give him access to fig1 and we build around what he is doing. Our goal is to make his job easier, not compete with him. If he helped build Peloton, he will immediately see where fig1 adds value.

Collaborative, not competitive
04

No lock-in

We do not want Hamilton House locked into fig1. We want to earn the work by continuing to make the platform better. If pieces belong to Moe or another partner, they should own them.

You stay in control
