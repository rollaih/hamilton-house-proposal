The Vision

Built for exactly this

After our conversation, we kept thinking about Hamilton House. The more we did, the more convinced we became — this is almost exactly the type of experience we built fig1 for.

Laird and Gabby have spent a lifetime learning. They are surrounded by extraordinary people — trainers, physicians, recovery experts, nutritionists — and what makes Hamilton House special is not that they claim to have every answer. It is that they know, trust, and learn from people who do.

Hamilton House can become the platform for those people. A trusted gateway to a world of health and performance — interactive, personalized, and connected to a real community. Not just content. A living, evolving experience that grows with every member interaction.

And all of it connects to the physical Hamilton House locations. Someone experiences Hamilton House at a resort in Hawaii or Oregon, goes home, continues the practice inside the app, stays connected to the community, books another visit — and continues learning from Laird, Gabby, and the people they trust.

That loop is the business. And fig1 is how you build it without starting over every time you learn something new.

Our Approach

A different kind of partnership

Hamilton House should not spend $70,000 to get an app framework. We built fig1 to eliminate that model entirely.

01

Get the initial experience live

Give us the content and we will get the initial Hamilton House experience up across web and mobile without the traditional build fee. It is an important showcase for us too. We earn the right to build more by delivering first.

02

Build the ongoing relationship

Our business together is built around the things that actually require people and expertise: content production and editing, new functionality, integrations, analytics, optimization, commerce, and whatever custom work Hamilton House needs next.

03

Work alongside Moe

If Moe is handling data infrastructure, analytics, or architecture, great — give him access to fig1 and we build around what he is doing. Our goal is to make his job easier, not compete with him. Fig1 and Moe could be much stronger together than either approach separately.

04

No lock-in

We do not want Hamilton House locked into fig1. We want to earn the work by continuing to make the platform better. If pieces belong to Moe or another partner, they should own them. We are here to accelerate, not control.
